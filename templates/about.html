About PrepBoosters

Our Mission

PrepBoosters was founded with a single mission: to make quality education accessible to every student preparing for competitive exams in India.

We understand the challenges faced by students preparing for JEE, NEET, and board exams. The high cost of study materials, lack of organized resources, and isolation during preparation can be overwhelming.

What We Offer
  • Curated study materials from trusted sources
  • Google Drive integration for easy access
  • Community platform for peer interaction
  • Regularly updated content
  • Completely free access
Our Team

We are a team of educators, engineers, and former toppers who have been through the exam preparation journey ourselves. We combine our experiences to create the best platform for current aspirants.
